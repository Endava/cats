# Special Fuzzers

CATS has 6 special Fuzzers (`FunctionalFlowFuzzer`, `FunctionalFuzzer`, `SecurityFuzzer`, `RandomFuzzer`, `TemplateFuzzer`, and `UserDictionary` fuzzers). They are named Fuzzers as a convention, but their
logic is not always related to fuzzing. Use the dedicated `cats run`,
`cats random`, and `cats template` commands as described in their pages.
